Privacy Policy

MRSV*Space Artist Tool — Unreal Engine editor plugin for environment artists

This application ("the tool") is an internal editor plugin that allows artists to package and upload 3D environments to the MRSV*Space platform via mod.io.

What we collect

  • Sign-in tokens: when you sign in with your Epic Games account, an authentication token issued by Epic Online Services is used to identify you to mod.io. The token is held only for the duration of your editor session on your own machine.
  • Content you upload: environments, metadata and images you explicitly publish through the tool are stored on mod.io under your account.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or tracking.
  • We do not store your Epic Games password; sign-in is handled entirely by Epic.

Third-party services

Sign-in and content hosting rely on Epic Games and mod.io, each governed by their own privacy policies.
